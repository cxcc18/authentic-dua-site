Privacy Policy

Authentic Dua for iOS · Effective 2 August 2026

Authentic Dua does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising.

No data collection

The app requires no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. It never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Fully offline

Authentic Dua works entirely offline and makes zero network requests. All content — the Quranic supplications, translations, and context notes — is bundled inside the app.

What stays on your device

The only information the app saves is what you create while using it:

  • Your saved favorites and the folders you organize them into
  • Your preferences (translation language and text sizes)

This information is stored only on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, the dua image or text is handed to the iOS share sheet, and where it goes is entirely your choice. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, changes are unlikely.
