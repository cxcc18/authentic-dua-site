Privacy Policy

Muhsin (formerly Authentic Dua) for iOS · Effective 14 October 2026

Muhsin has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it collects no personal data; the Qibla direction is worked out entirely on your phone. It has no ads and no third-party SDKs. Your favorites, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device.

What Muhsin sends

Quran audio is streamed or downloaded from a content delivery network when you play it; no account, no tracking, and no personal data is sent.

Muhsin sends your approximate location (about 1 km) to a prayer-times service to compute prayer times; it is not stored by Muhsin and no account, identifier or other personal data is sent.

Those are the only two things Muhsin sends anywhere. The name of the place shown in the header comes from Apple's own location services on your device.

Prayer times

Which service answers depends on where you are and the calculation authority you chose:

  • Outside Malaysia, and in Malaysia on any authority other than JAKIM: AlAdhan, with UmmahAPI as a fallback.
  • In Malaysia on JAKIM: waktusolat.app finds your JAKIM zone from the rounded location, then JAKIM's own e-Solat service gives the timetable for that zone. Only the zone code is sent for the timetable, with waktusolat.app as a fallback.
  • Your location is rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) before anything is sent. The exact position never leaves your device.
  • If you decline location access, you choose a city or prayer zone by name, and only that name or zone code is sent.
  • No account, device identifier or advertising identifier is sent, and no cookies are kept. As with any internet request, a service can see your IP address; what it keeps in its own logs is up to that service.
  • iOS may repeat the same request in the background to keep alerts current. Muhsin never takes a new location fix in the background.

Recitation audio

Audio comes from Tarteel's audio network (audio-cdn.tarteel.ai). When you play or download a recitation, your phone requests the file from that host, which can see your IP address. Nothing else is sent.

On your device

Favorites, bookmarks, your reading position, preferences, downloaded audio and the last prayer times fetched stay on your device and are deleted with the app. Prayer alerts are local notifications, and the lock-screen widget only shows times already stored on your device. Muhsin has no server, and the developer receives none of this data.

Your choices

Decline or withdraw location access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Muhsin, and choose a city or zone by name instead. Turn alerts off in Muhsin's Settings or in iOS Settings. Delete the app to remove everything it saved.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
