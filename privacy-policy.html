Privacy Policy

Muhsin (formerly Authentic Dua) for iOS · Effective 14 October 2026

Muhsin has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it collects no personal data; the Qibla direction is worked out entirely on your phone. It has no ads and no third-party SDKs. Your favorites, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device.

What Muhsin sends

Quran audio is streamed or downloaded from a content delivery network when you play it; no account, no tracking, and no personal data is sent.

Muhsin sends your approximate location (about 1 km) to a prayer-times service to compute prayer times; it is not stored by Muhsin and no account, identifier or other personal data is sent.

Those are the only two things Muhsin sends anywhere. The name of the place shown in the header comes from Apple's own location services on your device.

Prayer times

Prayer times come from AlAdhan, UmmahAPI, e-Solat JAKIM and waktusolat.app, depending on where you are. If you prefer not to share your location, choose a city or prayer zone by name instead.

Recitation audio

Audio is streamed from Tarteel's audio network (audio-cdn.tarteel.ai).

On your device

Favorites, bookmarks, your reading position, preferences, downloaded audio and the last prayer times fetched stay on your device and are deleted with the app. Muhsin has no server, and the developer receives none of this data.

Your choices

Decline or withdraw location access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Muhsin. Turn alerts off in Muhsin's Settings or in iOS Settings. Delete the app to remove everything it saved.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
