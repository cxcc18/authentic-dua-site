Privacy Policy

Muhsin (formerly Authentic Dua) for iOS · Version 2.0 · Effective 14 October 2026

Muhsin has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it collects no personal data; the Qibla direction is worked out entirely on your phone. Your favorites, bookmarks, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

What Muhsin does and does not send

Quran audio is streamed or downloaded from a content delivery network when you play it; no account, no tracking, and no personal data is sent.

Muhsin sends your approximate location (about 1 km) to a prayer-times service to compute prayer times; it is not stored by Muhsin and no account, identifier or other personal data is sent.

Those are the only two things Muhsin sends anywhere. It looks up the name of the place you are in through Apple's own location services on your device. Nothing else leaves your device.

Who makes Muhsin

Muhsin is made by Muhammad Azrin Zulbahrin, an independent developer. The developer runs no server for Muhsin and receives none of the data described on this page: requests go from your phone straight to the services named below. There is therefore nothing held by the developer to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

No data collection

The app requires no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. It never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Prayer times and the Qibla

Prayer times are published by a prayer-times service for the place you are in. Muhsin uses four, and which one answers depends only on where you are and which calculation authority you have chosen:

  • Outside Malaysia, and inside Malaysia on any authority other than JAKIM: AlAdhan, with UmmahAPI as an automatic fallback.
  • Inside Malaysia on JAKIM (the default there): JAKIM publishes its timetable per zone, so Muhsin asks waktusolat.app which of the 60 JAKIM zones you are in, and then reads the timetable for that zone from JAKIM's own e-Solat service, with waktusolat.app as an automatic fallback.

What is sent, in every case:

  • If you allow location access, your coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) before any request is made. The exact position never leaves the device.
  • Inside Malaysia the rounded coordinate goes only to the zone lookup, and once your zone is known only the zone code (for example "WLY01") is sent for the timetable — no coordinates at all. The answer is kept on your device and reused for up to thirty days, so the lookup is not repeated on every check.
  • If you decline, no location is used at all: you choose a city — or, in Malaysia, a prayer zone — by name instead, and only that city name or zone code is sent. A zone you choose yourself sends no location anywhere.
  • The request carries the location, city or zone, the date and your chosen calculation authority — nothing that identifies you or your device. The services do not receive an account, an advertising identifier or any other personal data from Muhsin, and Muhsin does not store your location anywhere except as the "last used" reference on your own device.
  • No cookies are kept. These services are reached over a connection that accepts no cookies and stores nothing between requests, so nothing that could identify you is sent back to them.
  • As with any request over the internet, the service that answers can see the network address (IP address) your connection uses. Muhsin adds nothing to it, and the developer cannot see it. What a service keeps in its own server logs is governed by that service, not by Muhsin; their sites are linked above.
  • The name shown in the header ("Kuala Lumpur") comes from Apple's reverse-geocoding service, built into iOS, and it is given the same rounded coordinate — never your exact position.
  • The Qibla direction is computed entirely on your device from your position and the coordinates of the Kaaba. No request is made for it.

Apple's App Store privacy label for this app therefore reports Coarse Location, used for App Functionality, not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Recitation audio

Recitations are served from the audio network of Tarteel, the publisher of the Quranic Universal Library. When you press play, or download a reciter, your phone asks that host for the audio file. The request contains the path of the file. Like any website, the host can see the network address of your connection; Muhsin sends it no account, identifier or other personal data.

Alerts, background refresh and the lock-screen widget

  • Prayer alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. They need your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings. Muhsin uses no push server and no push token.
  • The lock-screen widget shows prayer times already stored on your device. The app and the widget share a store inside your device (an app group); the widget makes no network requests at all.
  • Background refresh: iOS may occasionally wake Muhsin to top up the alerts. It repeats the same prayer-times request described above for the place you already chose. It never takes a new location fix in the background.

Your choices

  • Decline location access, or withdraw it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Muhsin. Choose a city or prayer zone by name instead.
  • Turn prayer alerts off in Muhsin's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Remove downloaded or cached recitation audio from within the app.
  • Delete the app to delete everything it saved on your device.

Reading works offline

Everything you read is bundled inside the app — the Quran text and Mushaf pages, the supplications, the translations, the tafsirs and the context notes — so reading works with no network at all. You can also download a reciter in full for offline listening, and the prayer times last fetched for your location or zone are kept on the device so the timetable still opens when you have no connection.

What stays on your device

The only information the app saves is what you create while using it:

  • Your saved favorites and the folders you organize them into
  • Your Quran bookmarks and where you last stopped reading
  • Your preferences (theme, translation language, reciter, and text sizes)
  • Recitation audio you have downloaded or that has been cached while streaming
  • The prayer timetable last fetched for your location or zone, and the zone last looked up for you, so the timetable still opens offline

This information is stored only on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

Where the content comes from

The Mushaf layout, page fonts and Arabic script are from the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex (QPC v4). Translations, tafsirs and recitations are from the Quranic Universal Library (QUL, qul.tarteel.ai). Prayer times come from the services named above. The full list is in the app under Settings, About & privacy, Credits. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which this page does not cover.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, the dua image or text is handed to the iOS share sheet, and where it goes is entirely your choice. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
